Facilities

SCIENCE LABORATORIES

There are Laboratories for Physics, Chemistry & Biology with adequate number of Apparatus, sufficient Chemical, Slides, Microscope, OHP, LCD Projector, Herbarium and Specimen for study. A Maths Lab, Language Lab and Junior Science Lab are also set up.

LIBRARY

The School has well equipped library and all students are its member. The School library is presently subscribing five Hindi and English Newspapers, three monthly magazines and is likely to increase their number in coming future.

Rules are:
- Students are encouraged to use the school library under the guidance of Teacher / Librarian.
- Damaged books must brought to the notice of the librarian at the time of issuing the books.
- Library books should be returned before the due date mentioned at the time of issuing the book, failing of which imposed Rs.2/ fine per day after due date.
- In case student loses / damages the issued book, the same shall be deposited by the student in the library.

COMPUTER LAB

The importance of Computer Technology support teaching and learning, develop student IT caliber, or to support educational management, is now recognized throughout the world. Keeping this in view Unity Public Senior Secondary School is equipped with a High-Tech Computer-Lab and well qualified faculties. Students are provided with the following facilities: Latest Multimedia PCs
high speed Internet Connectivity
Computer Based Training Programme.
A huge Collection of Educational-Software.
Computer Education to all students.
Computer Projects developed by the Teaching staff members.
Computer-Science as an elective subject at +1 and +2 level.

MEDICAL FACILITIES

A First Aid Kit is kept in the school to attend the student in need.
Parents are requested to intimate the principal in case their wards are allergic to a particular medicine.
The students suffering from infectious diseases should not be sent to school before the complete recovery and same must be intimated to the school.
