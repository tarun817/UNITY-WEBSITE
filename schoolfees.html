Withdrawals

The School authority has right to compel the parents to withdraw their children on the following grounds:

  • Irregular attendance and school charges.
  • Unsatisfactory academic progress.
  • Bad Conduct.
  • Transfer certificate will be released only on clearance of all school dues and RS.75/- shall be charges for school leaving certificate.

Fee Regulation

  • The monthly charges are payable before 12th of every month.
  • Every charge will be paid before the due date.
  • After due date Rs. 10/ per day fine will be paid with the addition of charges
  • Charges once paid will neither be refunded nor be adjusted.
  • No transfer certificate will be issued unless all dues have been paid.
  • No charges are refundable in case of provisional admission in 9th, 11th and 12th classes if students fail in previous classes.
  • Pupils will not be allowed to appear terminal examination as well as Board examination unless all charges have been paid.
  • 1/3rd of admission charges will be paid on readmission in case of students struck off for any reason.
